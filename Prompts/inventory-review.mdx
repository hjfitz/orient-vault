# Inventory review

A keep-or-drop pass over everything open, oldest first, that comes back as one table you
can act on rather than a summary you have to re-read. Untick anything out of scope; every
item that stays gets a verdict, exactly once.

## Ask
Return one table, one row per inventory item, every item exactly once, with these columns: id, item, verdict, because, next action, owner.
The verdict is one of KEEP, DROP, ACT, DEFER. No others, no hedging, no blanks.
"because" is one line and must refer to the keep criterion above.
"next action" is a single concrete step, or an em dash for DROP.
Then, under the table:
- **Do first** — the three ACT rows in the order they should happen, and why that order.
- **Drop entirely** — the DROP rows written as one sentence I could send to someone.
- **Not enough information** — any row you could not judge, and the one fact that would judge it.
Never invent an item, never merge two items into one row, and never quietly leave one out.`}

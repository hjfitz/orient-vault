# Plan intent

The self-contained brief a fresh planning agent needs: intent, the repo to read, and the
service overview. It withholds known-context, the work ledger and the explainers on
purpose — that read is worth having precisely because the vault's history has not
coloured it, and the cross-check comes afterwards. Untick every service but the one.

## Ask
Read the real source at that repo and follow it where it actually leads.
1. The critical files and boundaries this touches.
2. A numbered sequence of increments, each independently implementable and verifiable, with the verification named inline. Prefer three to seven; if it needs more, say the work is too big to be one plan.
3. The trade-offs you considered, and why you rejected the ones you rejected.
State every assumption you had to make as an assumption.

## What has been withheld, and why
You have the service overview and nothing else. Its known-context, work ledger and explainers exist and are being kept from you on purpose: this read is valuable because the vault's own history has not biased it, and that cross-check runs after you report. Do not ask for them, and do not guess at what they say.`}

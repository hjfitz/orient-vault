The timer is one file, `<vault>/.knowledge/focus/state.json`, served by a `focus` nav-status provider, so the rail row on any page, the page and every tab share one clock. A phase ends on the server (whoever reads it first after `endsAt`), which logs the finished block to `focus/YYYY-MM.jsonl`, tagged with a "Working on" task. Editable lengths, opt-in notifications (per browser), Today and Last 7 days views, a Music section (mixes in `.knowledge/config.yaml` under `focus.music`, YouTube embed, click-to-load), and synthesised ambient layers (rain, wind, waves, brown noise; Web Audio, independent volumes, layer over music or play alone). `/ux-review` pass: three-tier grid, one shared clock, state as text. Music and ambient persist across pages: a sound store and a `SoundHost` in the root layout own the audio engine and the one YouTube iframe (laid over a slot on /focus, docked bottom-right elsewhere; never moved in the tree, so it never reloads), and the Focus rail row names what plays and stops it. Music follows the timer (plays while a focus block runs, pauses on breaks; the player's own state comes back through its message channel) and the mixes are rows. Done early (`done` command): logs the time actually worked as a finished task, starts the break, clears the task and steps, no chime; Today and the week show a done chip and count. Ideate steps: a title in Working on becomes steps via a tool-less Sonnet run (~$0.03), grounded in the ticket's Jira text when the title names one, as a proposal to untick then use/add/discard. Ideate now looks for context first (Jira, vault notes about the ticket or matching the title, ledger, open PRs, digest; sensitive/excluded paths filtered once) and every step cites the numbered sources it rests on, shown as chips and a Sources fold; the cited pages travel into the timer's checklist as links. A /ux-review of /focus moved the chips under the step text (they squeezed text to a word a line at 390px) and gave them 28px phone targets. Entries are editable: each row in Today (and an Earlier-this-week fold) opens in place to change task, minutes and finished, or delete behind a confirm; only that entry's line in its month file is rewritten, atomically. Pick a task: a headless tool-less Sonnet run (~$0.05) over the digest's open Needs me gives up to six tasks with steps as a swipeable deck; taking one sets the timer's task and a steps checklist.

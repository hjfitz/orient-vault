# Design, spike and build a meeting transcriber for orient

## Outcome
`orient-scribe.service` (Go, `scribe/`, 127.0.0.1:3013) runs record, transcribe (sherpa-onnx, local), Opus and a tool-less Claude summary. orient's `/recordings` (`g w`) records from a title or the meeting on now or next, lists every recording with its tl;dr, and shows each one's summary, speaker naming and a seekable transcript. orient commits: `30a52f8` `97f770e` `7ea50b6` `d2a233e` `48565a1` `dc4a326` `b044e47`.

Verified by driving the deployed page with a simulated call on virtual devices: done about 37s after stop, for £0.03. AMI scores DER 13.1% and WER 20.7%. Level normalisation closed the recorder's accuracy gap. Still owed: a real Teams call (headset switching to call mode, behaviour while muted).

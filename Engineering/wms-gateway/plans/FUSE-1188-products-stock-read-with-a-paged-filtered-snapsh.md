## Intent
<!-- What we are changing and why. Link the ticket. -->
[FUSE-1188](https://globalfreight.atlassian.net/browse/FUSE-1188), under epic FUSE-792; taken over from Nylah on 2026-10-02 (she is on FUSE-1211/1076, Harry: "I'll cover those both"). Serve a business's product stock from SNAP as canonical `type: "product"` stock positions with the ILG filters applied, a SKU filter and pagination, fresh to within about 5 minutes, and with an upstream failure distinguishable from an empty result. It delivers alongside [[plans/FUSE-1036-kits-with-derived-potentialkitqty-in-the-stock-r|FUSE-1036]]: this plan owns the shared SNAP stock read, the snapshot, the list contract and the `ForSKU` signature, and 1036's kit increments (K0–K2) stack on it. Proof: a local run of the real gateway pages through a business's full stock on GADG (real SNAP) and HJF (the simulator), with totals matching an independent SNAP read and a stock change showing up within the freshness bound.

- Delivered with [[plans/FUSE-1036-kits-with-derived-potentialkitqty-in-the-stock-r|the FUSE-1036 plan]], whose K0–K2 stack after increment 8 here. Its Harry-confirmed rules carry over unchanged: SNAP's own quantity formula for kit availability, nested kits handled fully, closed lines ignored, a zero-quantity line or a component failing the filter makes a kit 0, a kit whose own SKU fails the filter is excluded.
- Real SNAP rows are one per SKU × Site × **Facility** × Owner × Status × State, with no unique row id: of GADG's 103 W/00 rows only 93 are distinct without Facility (TTRS3 has `2SP` and `2SP_REWORK` in one site). Live spike 2026-10-02 and snap-adapter#248.
- `$filter=BizId eq 'GADG' and Status eq '00' and State eq 'W'` works (103 rows); `OwnerId eq 'GADG'` gives 100 — 3 rows have BizId GADG and another owner. Spike 2026-10-02.
- A filtered list matching nothing is `200 {"Status":"Success","Message":"Successes: 0/0","Results":[]}`, also for a `$skip` past the end, so stopping on an empty page is safe. Spike 2026-10-02.
- `$orderby=SKUId,Site,Facility,OwnerId` works with `$skip`; `$orderby=LineId` is a 400 (the wire field is `LineID`, null on every row). `$top` 100/500/1000 all accepted; the real cap can't be found on dev (GADG has 103 W/00 rows, the host 173). Spike 2026-10-02.
- One 103-row page is about 122 KB and 0.15–0.3 s. Spike 2026-10-02.
- **37 of GADG's 112 products have no stock-total row at all**; 79 SKUs have W/00 rows and 54 of those sum to Qty 0. Kits are products with `Group2 = MASTERSKU` (10 on GADG, 7 with stock rows). Spike 2026-10-02 (this session) and #248.
- The dev SNAP host is shared across businesses (2 BizIds unfiltered), as prod hosts are (INC-36), so every read filters by business. Spike and vault.
- On `main` the config-service path doubles `/api/v1`, so dev backend resolution 404s until #7 (`9b400f8`) merges; a local run on `main` needs `CONFIG_SERVICE_BASE_URL` without `/api/v1`, on a #7-based branch with it. Source read.
- Nothing can invoke the deployed dev gateway (internal ingress, no invoker binding), so every live check here is a **local run** of the real binary. Source read; [[known-context]].
- The adapterclient LRU is 1024 entries per business client with a 30 min TTL and no per-request opt-out; its singleflight key ignores any such flag. Source read.
- Widening `normalizeUnions` to every schema would turn `SnapShipment.CustomerOrder` into a pointer that `ptr.FromAny[string]` silently reads as nil (no test covers it), so the nullable collapse is scoped to the new roots only. Source read. Supersedes the agent's earlier 1036 UNRESOLVED 6 decision.
- `tools/canonschema/src/index.ts` only looks names up in `@gfs-bifrost/types`; `@gfs-bifrost/snap/types` also exports a different `SnapProductSchema`, so the second lookup must never shadow a `types` name. Source read.
- Cloud Run: 60 s request timeout, 512Mi, max 5 instances, 4 SNAP slots per instance. SNAP client: 30 s per attempt, up to 6 attempts (~183 s worst case), so the snapshot build needs its own deadline. Source read.
- The per-business circuit breaker is shared with sales-order reads; FUSE-1195's stock sync caps consecutive failures one below the threshold for the same reason. #248.
- No live check can prove UNLINK or non-W exclusion on GADG (no such rows); HJF fixtures prove it on the simulator only. 1036 increment 1(b).

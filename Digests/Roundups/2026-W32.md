## Final approved text (Slack-native formatting)

*Fuse this week* — w/c 3 August

Happy Friday team — a proper shipping week, plus a sharp mid-week pivot for Purdy & Figg handled without dropping a beat. Here's the wrap:

*🚀 Product progress*
• Work orders took their biggest step yet: Fuse now generates a Finished Good Receipt when a work order completes, operators can configure how each customer receives the two work-order output files, and a failed warehouse submission now retries automatically instead of sticking. The delivery step itself (writing the files, firing the webhooks) is built and in review — no customer receives these yet.
• Pick/Pack confirmations completed their final build item (the outbound mapping settings) — the capability is fully built and ready to enter QA.

*⚙️ Engineering*
• The observability rollout completed: seven services now report the same standard operational metrics in production, giving us fleet-wide dashboards and much wider visibility in one step.
• Every failure event now names the connector and transaction that produced it, and warehouse rejections carry the real error message instead of an opaque code — triage goes straight to the cause.
• The service that gatekeeps every inbound record was hardened: malformed input and unexpected errors no longer restart it mid-stream, and several admin and metrics gaps were closed.

*📈 Reliability & operations*
• No new incidents this week. Production held steady: ~10,800 requests through the instrumented services with 29 failures recorded, all on a single connector. (Metric coverage itself widened this week with the observability rollout.)
• Returns confirmation files missing return and expiry dates were root-caused to a shared library silently dropping date fields — fix published.
• Three new high-severity dependency advisories tripped our security gate across the fleet mid-week — exactly what it's there for. Triage is in progress and a proven fix pattern is rolling out repo by repo.

*💡 Highlights*
• Matt dropped tools to own the design and development of Shopify bulk order ingest end-to-end — validated against real throughput numbers and turned into build-ready work within the week.
• Harry H carried the support load — owning the confirmations over-sending escalation through to an agreed cutover plan and root-causing two customer file issues same-day — while also delivering the enhanced replay queue (grouped retries, a replay audit trail, claim/dismiss workflow), now in review.
• Tom and Alex kept the quality bar moving all week — systematic test passes across FUSE, surfacing issues before customers hit them.

*🤝 Cross-team & business*
• The switch to per-customer dynamic confirmation routing is ready: ops configured outbound mappings for all six affected customers, and the flip is deliberately scheduled for early next week rather than a Friday.
• A new guide — "Investigating issues in Datadog & escalating to the dev team" — was published for the ILG support team, moving first-line investigation closer to self-serve.
• Customer testing progressed on two fronts: Purdy & Figg's Shopify app UAT has test orders flowing in dev, and Lisa Eldridge PL is testing live through the Poland DC, with the one config bug found fixed the same day.

*🔭 Up next*
• Deliver kit confirmations for Purdy & Figg ahead of their kit stock arriving in the warehouse next week — rescoped at short notice, and the fix for kit orders breaking confirmations is already landing across connectors.
• Scale the Shopify connector for Purdy & Figg's volumes with bulk order ingest — the design was validated this week against real throughput numbers, lifting the current ~1,700 orders/hour ceiling.
• Deliver work-order outputs to customers end-to-end — writing the files and firing the webhooks that complete the journey.

*📊 At a glance*
• 46 PRs merged (excluding bots)
• 5 Jira items done, 8 progressed
• 0 new incidents
• ~10,800 prod API requests through the instrumented services; 29 failures recorded (single connector)
• 2,238 records processed in prod (steady-state level with last week — last week's headline total was a one-off bulk backfill)

Massive effort all round — have a great weekend 🍻

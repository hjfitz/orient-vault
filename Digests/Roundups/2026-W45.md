# Fuse this week — w/c 2 November 2026

Drafted Fri 6 Nov (data to about 16:45 UTC). Harry posted it himself. This is the draft text as approved for him; his intro and sign-off, and any edits he made when pasting, are not captured here.

## Final text

**Fuse this week** — w/c 2 November

**🚀 Product progress**
- Shopify stock sync and the customer Get Stock API both entered QA this week. Neither is live for customers yet.
- Work Orders stayed in QA, and batch and expiry details now carry through finished-good receipts and work-order confirmations. Two defects cleared QA, two more are in QA, and one is ready to enter QA.
- Customer Transaction History moved forward. The API and the Shopify bulk-ingest path now keep exactly what a customer sent, so support will be able to see it. Showing it to support is still to come.

**⚙️ Engineering**
- The central config store every service reads from now runs on managed Postgres in prod. We froze writes, copied the data and checked it against the old store before switching, and the old setup is kept as a way back.
- Customer SFTP traffic now goes through a load-balanced, three-instance host instead of a single VM. The old VM is out of the serving path, and the connector no longer manages SFTP users itself.
- Snap outage protection is now one shared, flag-gated package that the adapter and poller use, with metrics and alerts, in place of two separate copies. 18 services also moved onto the standard runtime images, and there's a new shared Go library repo.

**📈 Reliability & operations**
- No new incidents this week. Last week's follow-ups are still being closed out.
- Platform failures in prod were down 91% (36 vs 389 in the same days last week). The prod API gateway handled ~2,950 requests with no failures recorded.
- One thing open: Tracking Service started hitting a database transaction-expiry error early this morning, and its alerts fired several times through the day. The cause is still open.

**💡 Highlights**
- Nylah finished the Fuse UI refresh. The new Order Tracker and Retry Queue have been live in prod since Tuesday with no errors, giving support a proper work queue.
- Schuster made batch and expiry data flow through work-order and finished-goods confirmations, which EDI and customer receivers need. It was also a big jump in output on last week.
- Matt made three services (dedupe, the API and the Shopify connector) stop hanging when Redis goes down, and they now log it.

**🤝 Cross-team & business**
- Purdy & Figg's sale day held up. About 100k orders were picked up by 12:48 and nothing was dropped, with all monitors green.
- Escentric Molecules went live on Fuse (announced Tuesday): UK off Jitterbit, a new Poland operation alongside, and 500+ orders into Snap in week one.
- Snap latency for ILG IT is back to ~100-200 ms, and Datadog now posts into #snap-wms so IT and the team see when Snap is unhealthy. The new #fuse-peak channel is the place for peak-weekend issues.

**🔭 Up next**
- Take stock sync and the Get Stock API through QA towards their first customers.
- Refine the Jitterbit parity work: the order and confirmation controls customers still rely on.
- Get what a customer sent in front of support.

**📊 At a glance** (Mon to Fri afternoon)
- 144 PRs merged, plus 55 dependency bumps
- 18 Jira items done (6 were epics closed in a board tidy-up), 15 progressed
- 0 new incidents
- ~2,950 prod API gateway requests, no failures recorded
- ~159,000 events handled in prod (↑17%), ~8,650 of them new records (↓14%)
- 11 security-fix PRs

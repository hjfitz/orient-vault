## Final text

**Fuse this week** — w/c 21 September

Happy Friday all. I'm off today so this one's pre-scheduled. It was a big week for support tooling, and Transaction History in particular should make life easier for anyone chasing "did it arrive?" questions.

**🚀 Product progress**
- Transaction History now lets support find any record by its reference, shows every record type (returns, work orders, pick/pack and finished-goods confirmations), and gives each row a real status. It's live in prod and was announced to ILG support on Thursday.
- Customer mappings can now use lookup tables. Storage and the SFTP and Shopify connectors are live, and the editing screen is shipped behind a flag.
- Smart Confirmation Routing's connector changes cleared QA: each confirmation now goes only to the connector it belongs to, and the old business-level confirmations switch is gone. The Template Library's main screen also cleared QA.
- Shopify stock sync is underway (the schedule, the export storage and a new egress service are in place, with the sync trigger in review), and so is the Stock Position API (the stock data model and the warehouse gateway service are done).

**⚙️ Engineering**
- The confirmation poller now only polls businesses that have confirmations switched on (14 of 30 in prod) and skips work that's already claimed, which cuts wasted polling across the fleet.
- New monitoring on Pub/Sub delivery headroom and backlog drain, the Platform Health dashboard now points at the right metrics, and SFTP validation failures (which were hidden in prod logs) now surface with an alert.
- Failed confirmations now land in the Retry Queue as individual retryable entries instead of being lumped together or dropped, and a retry keeps the record's original source.

**📈 Reliability & operations**
- No new incidents this week. Monitor alerts fell about 70% on last week (93 vs 317) and platform failures in prod were down 84% (330 vs 2,088), both Mon–Thu against the same span last week.
- Fixed in prod: stock adjustments reaching connectors that have them switched off (hit Purdy & Figg), a warehouse timeout that duplicated kit lines, a missing database index behind a recurring performance alert, and the permission SAIRA's Shopify order tagging needs.
- Fuse had been picking up confirmations for a few customers still on the old integration (Dr Sam's, Lisa Eldridge, Shackleton); these are being switched off connector by connector with ILG.

**💡 Highlights**
- Harry Humby fixed a bug where one unmappable shipment blocked every goods-out confirmation for a business, and scoped stock-adjustment records by business so two customers can no longer overwrite each other's data.
- Matt adopted one structured-logging standard across the services, making logs consistent to search in Datadog, and stood up the new Shopify egress service that stock sync will run on.
- Schuster spotted Purdy & Figg order failures early and worked them through with ILG to a carrier setup gap in the warehouse system, so the orders were replayed the same day.

**🤝 Cross-team & business**
- A heavy support week: 133 Euro Parfums orders stuck after the Snap maintenance were resent, a 25-day Byoma goods-in failure was traced to its mapping config, and Sportslux's "silent" file failure now alerts.
- The Transaction History launch landed well with ILG. Their ask for a CSV export is already on the backlog.
- Dock & Bay is provisionally going live on Tuesday 29 September, with a dedicated channel set up for it.

**🔭 Up next**
- Keep Shopify stock in step with the warehouse automatically.
- Let customers query live stock positions over the API.

**📊 At a glance** (Mon–Thu)
- 79 PRs merged, plus 38 dependency bumps
- 28 Jira items delivered, 15 progressed
- 0 new incidents
- ~3,500 prod API gateway requests, no failures recorded
- ~75,000 records processed in prod (↓ 20% on last week)

Thanks all for a strong week. Have a good weekend, and I'll see you Monday.

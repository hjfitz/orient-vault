## Final text

**Fuse this week** — w/c 5 October

**🚀 Product progress**
- Shopify stock sync is taking shape. Operators can now save and validate a sync schedule on a connector, Fuse can pull stock from the warehouse and export it, and every sync run is recorded. Failure reporting, replay and visibility in Transaction History are built and in review. It isn't live for any customer yet.
- The Stock Position API's warehouse gateway now serves product and kit stock straight from Snap, with per-customer rate limiting. Kit stock is ready to enter QA. The customer-facing endpoint is still in progress.
- Kit orders now confirm back to Shopify.
- Support has a new Order Tracker in Fuse UI: a work queue grouped by customer, linking through to the Retry Queue and Transaction History. It launched on Tuesday.

**⚙️ Engineering**
- The confirmation poller now clears Snap's outbound queue itself once a confirmation is published. It's on for 7 prod businesses, up from none, with zero failed clears in the soak.
- Shopify connector and egress moved to Admin API 2026-04 ahead of Shopify's 16 October cutoff, and the connector and the Shopify app now share one token-refresh implementation instead of two copies.

**📈 Reliability & operations**
- No new Fuse incidents. The prod API gateway handled ~3,100 requests with no failures recorded (last week: 33 failures in ~3,000). Total platform failures in prod were down 46% (2,234 vs 4,105).
- Two things went the other way: new-record failures rose from 95 to 311, and Shopify confirmation-delivery alerts fired 58 times against 12. Seven SAIRA orders that looped because the warehouse added extra lines were cleared by hand.
- Fixed in prod: failures carrying wrong-typed values were being dropped instead of stored (Tuesday's data-loss alert); they're now kept and retryable.
- A critical proxy-addr security advisory, plus a handful of others, cleared across five services (8 PRs).

**💡 Highlights**
- Nylah gave the new stock gateway response caching and live config updates, so a changed Snap credential is picked up, and added the shared failure type that stock sync reports through.
- Harry Humby built the stock export that Shopify stock sync runs on, added monitors for runs that publish nothing or leave a kit out, and fixed the replay data-loss bug.
- Schuster fixed two mapping bugs in the standard SFTP template (goods-in stock status and goods-out line number pointed at the wrong fields), and built the stock sync schedule screen.

**🤝 Cross-team & business**
- Support asks answered: Fuse has no limits on customer credential characters (for the new Snap password rules), and the looping SAIRA orders were traced to extra warehouse lines.
- Migration pipeline: Jitterbit migrations freeze over peak. Phlur is still targeting 26 Oct, Jo Loves EU has slipped to 12 Nov, and Sam McKnight can't migrate until stock sync is in Fuse.
- Jack thanked Harry Humby for the Shopify retrieval-conditions "Test conditions" button, which helps L2 and projects set up brands.

**🔭 Up next**
- Take Shopify stock sync to its first live run.
- Extend stock sync to SFTP-connector customers (planning Monday).

**📊 At a glance** (Mon to Fri 3pm)

- 15 Jira items done, 20 progressed
- 0 new Fuse incidents
- ~3,100 prod API gateway requests, no failures recorded
- ~76,000 events handled in prod (↓29%), ~6,900 of them new records (↓10%)

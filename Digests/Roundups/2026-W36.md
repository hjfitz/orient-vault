## Final text

*Fuse this week* — w/c 31 August

Happy Friday — a big week, mostly heads-down on foundations rather than customer-facing features, plus a live incident that got caught and fixed same-day.

🚀 *Product progress*
• The Mapping Template Library reached feature-complete this week — customers can now save, browse, and reuse mapping configurations as templates instead of rebuilding them per connector.
• Smart Confirmation Routing advanced on multiple fronts: the per-connector confirmations toggle shipped, and the pieces that let Shopify subscribe to and broadcast confirmations by source moved into code review.

⚙️ *Engineering*
• Stood up the entire Fuse Datadog/observability estate as Terraform this week — catalog, dashboards, monitors, and alert routing are now managed as code, so a new service gets proper monitoring and the right on-call routing from one line of config instead of manual setup.
• Cleared a fleet-wide security/dependency-audit backlog across roughly a dozen repos, closing out the base-image CVE that had nightly builds red most of the week.
• Continued moving dedupe-service onto its new framework — two more request paths migrated this week.

📈 *Reliability & operations*
• One incident this week: a P2 where Shopify started rejecting stock-adjustment updates for two customers. Root-caused and fixed the same afternoon, verified in prod; one manual backfill remains.
• No monitors went into alert all week. No failures were recorded on the Fuse API gateway in prod across just over 3,000 requests.
• Platform failure volume in prod dropped sharply week over week (from ~3,400 to ~660), even as ingest volume rose sharply on continued go-live traffic.

💡 *Highlights*
• Nylah singlehandedly cleared the fleet-wide dependency-audit backlog — one fix per repo across eight repos — and led today's incident to a same-day fix.
• Harry Humby fixed a retry-queue bug that was silently changing an edited value's type — the exact site-ID mangling issue found during this week's Purdy & Figg go-live.

🤝 *Cross-team & business*
• Purdy & Figg's go-live went out clean on Monday — the CEO sent a note of thanks to the team.
• Fielding ILG's growing questions on Datadog access and dashboard ownership now more of their team is live on it.

🔭 *Up next*
• Move the Template Library through QA.
• Push Smart Confirmation Routing's remaining pieces through review.
• Move goods-out parcel-data fidelity fixes into QA.

📊 *At a glance*
• 59 PRs merged
• 0 Jira items done, 12 progressed
• 1 new incident (P2, fixed same day)
• ~3,100 API requests through the instrumented gateway, no failures recorded
• ~38,000 records processed in prod (↑ from ~4,800 last week)
• 14 CVE/dependency-audit PRs merged across 2 fleet-wide waves

Have a good weekend all.

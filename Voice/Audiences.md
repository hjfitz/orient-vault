## People with no data

Internal and friendly: use **Wider**. A new external contact or a customer's people: use **Channels**,
ILG-facing, and ask Harry if it is not obvious. Do not guess a nickname: pet names (pal, bud, mate, luv,
fella) are only for people in the tables above who get them. No nickname is also fine.

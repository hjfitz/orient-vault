# Up (Oli Denton, John Clarke)

Part of [[Voice]]. Readers: [[Audiences]]. Built from 580 messages (Oli DM 463, John Clarke DM 16, group DMs
with John and/or Oli and Darren or Stef 101). **This is the thinnest register.** 56% of the Oli DM is banter,
gym, music and personal chat. The "reporting upward" voice is visible mostly in about 80 rows to or with John
(CTO), and the asks, risk flags and pushback each have about five examples or fewer.

> **Calibration warning:** the exemplars below were chosen to show how he writes, and about 40 to 50% of them
> carry an emoji. Real replies carry one far less often (see the emoji budget in [[Voice]]). Copy the shape and
> wording, not the emoji density. The stock wrappers in them (thanks, cheers, "shout if you need anything",
> "I'll look in to it") are also over-represented: see "Substance first" in [[Voice]] for the real rates.

## Shape

- **With Oli:** the terse shape ([[Registers/peers-terse]]). Median 7 words, 73% lowercase starts, 0 full stops,
  `!` 1%, emoji 5%. He does not become formal for his manager. Work rows are longer (median 9) and more
  capitalised than banter rows (median 5). Bursts of 3+ messages: 67% of the DM. He thinks aloud in fragments.
- **With John, or in a group with him:** tidier. Lowercase starts fall to 44 to 51% (the Oli DM is 73%),
  `!` 9% in groups and 25% in the tiny DM, emoji 18% in groups, **no swearing** (0 of 81 rows). Greets:
  `Hello gents!`, `Hello!`.
- Terminal punctuation: 80% no punctuation at all.
- Thanks to Oli carry a nickname (pal 7, luv 2, fella 2), none to John. `Thanks!` and `Cheers!` to John.
- No sign-offs ("thanks for your time", "speak soon" are 0). Threads close on `sound`, `np`, `ta`, or silence.

## Moves, with exemplars

**Report a result unprompted, then move on.** Result first, detail second, a smiley at the end.
- "Managed to nab a sweet domain for some temporary prod hosting :smile: <https://fuse.ilg.software/login>"
- "however, our pipeline templates are looking excellent :raised_hands:"
- "somebody said our swagger docs site wasn't good, and we took that personally :smile:"
- "Logging changes were merged midday yesterday and you can see the impact on the gcp billing dashboard; i've asked matt to build ours out with finer detail fwiw. Got the networking changes going in today so we should see things reduce more as the weeks go on :thumbsup:"
- "Just caught the team up on this - they're good with it and like the direction, which is great. doing some async planning in <#C09PBES3MHT|bifrost-forge>"

**Ask for a decision or a resource.** The ask is explicit, the why comes with it, and a next step is already
taken. Often phrased as a past intention, not "can I".
- "Wanted to get the greenlight from you as it's your org you, before I started speaking to people and moving our shared workflows and terraform modules to a different github org etc."
- "Is it possible to move Matt/HH/Schuster's clanker license to cursor, away from copilot? They're butting the token limit all the time it seems"
- "It's still deployed to the bifrost gcp. Is it possible to get a project for platform engineering work at all? It'd mean that we could start work on rolling out golden images too :raised_hands:"
- Follow-up when the answer is ambiguous: "Excellent :raised_hands:. Was more thinking who creates the project but that clarification helps too!"
- Close-out: "I've put a request in with SRE to get the project created :slightly_smiling_face:"

**Ask for time.** Soft and short. Never "urgent".
- "Do you smart people have a minute later today?"
- "fancy a walk and talk tuesday?"
- "Did you wanna leadership catch-up - or want to take a breath instead?"
- "shoot the huddle req my way whenever works"

**Flag a risk.** A plain description of the mismatch, a dry tag (`lol`, `tbf`), no alarm words.
- "going from early november to 2 weeks from now is a little too bold for us lol"
- "tbf him taking a look in to things is pretty nice and helpful, it's just a little misplaced"

**Agree.**
- "yeah this looks good" / "Yeah shouldn't be a peg" / "Aha thats a really good call" / "Absolutely sick!"

**Decline or defer.**
- "Not today, too early for the new starts"
- "Absolutely. Maybe next week as I'd like to include some of the ILG team too"
- "i'm not, unfortunately"

**Narrow a scope so it does not read as an instruction.**
- "Ah to clarify - I'm thinking largely for the work on ilg-platform as it's our innersourcing world. I won't tell you how to build things in the ECM world :slightly_smiling_face:"

**Give a view on a proposal, mildly.**
- "you and darren should be admins over the ecm projects imo" then "team owns project"

**Offer help.**
- "If you were going to migrate and wanted an eye, happy to muck in"
- "can give you the raw and non llm-refined notes too if that helps?"

**Protect their time.**
- "I put in a meeting but it can be a message, so ignore it" then "save you some admin lol"

**Praise.** Short, from a peer angle, rare.
- "Darren - UI toolkit is absolutely mint :ok_hand:"

**Reply to John when he says something is fine.**
- "Fab! Does this live with SRE?"
- "Hello! Ah - no hassles about the EntraID stuff. We could probably handle it through an email/group chat thinking about it :slightly_smiling_face:"

## Lexicon

yeah (22 openers), ah/ahh, sound, cheers, ta, fab, mint, `clanker` (AI, from about Apr 2026), `tl;dr is that ...`
(once), `Wanted to ... before I ...`, `Might be of note - ...`, `not sure how well known this is, but ...`,
`Did you wanna ... - or want to ... instead?` (an offer with an out). Hedges in 37 of 580 rows: should, might,
I think, tbh, tbf, imo, fwiw.

## Do not

- No closing sign-off. No "thanks for your time".
- No strong swearing to John or in a group with him. With Oli it is rare (11 of 580 rows, all in the 1:1 DM).
- No bulleted status update (1 in 580, and it was a vent). Long messages are run-on sentences joined by ` - `,
  `;` or `, so`, or are split over 2 to 4 short messages.
- No exclamation-mark enthusiasm to Oli ("Great news!!" would be wrong). `!` is for greetings and John.
- Do not carry the Oli-DM banter (gym, memes, links) into a message to John or a group.
- No "urgent", no air-cover phrasing. The nearest real thing is the greenlight ask above.
- No formal incident or escalation voice: there are 0 samples, so flag it instead of inventing it.
